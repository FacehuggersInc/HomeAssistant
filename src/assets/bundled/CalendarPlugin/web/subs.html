The iCal address, not the "add this calendar" link beside it — that one is a web page and has no events in it. A Google ?cid= link is converted for you.

    Where do I get the address?

    Google - Settings for that calendar, then Integrate calendar, then the address ending basic.ics. Public and secret are both there; the link above them opens Google rather than the feed.
    Apple - share the calendar publicly and copy the webcal link.
    Outlook - publish the calendar and choose ICS.

    Treat it as a password. Anyone holding it can read that calendar.

    Google caches this feed for hours, so a change made on your phone will not appear here straight away.
